# NimbusCart Ticket Triage Guidelines (internal)

## Categories
- Shipping: delivery delays, tracking, lost parcels, address changes.
- Refund & Returns: returns, refunds, exchanges, refund status.
- Account & Login: passwords, OTP, locked accounts, profile changes, suspicious logins.
- Product: broken or defective items, replacements, warranty, product questions.
- Billing & Payments: charges, duplicate payments, invoices, coupons, failed payments.
- Other: anything not listed above, such as bulk or corporate requests, partnerships, general feedback.

## Urgency rules
- High: legal threats, fraud or unauthorised activity, money charged more than once, safety hazards, orders more than 7 days late, or a customer who says they contacted us several times without a reply.
- Medium: a late order, an overdue refund, a broken product, cannot log in.
- Low: general questions, how-to questions, information requests.

## Sentiment rules
- Angry: threats, demands such as "fix this now", insults, words like "worst" or "never again", text in capitals.
- Frustrated: disappointment, impatience, a repeated problem.
- Neutral: a plain question or request.
- Positive: thanks or praise.

## Risk flags
- legal_threat: lawyer, court, consumer forum, legal action.
- fraud: unauthorised orders or charges, hacked account, scam.
- payment_dispute: chargeback, contacting the bank, charged multiple times.
