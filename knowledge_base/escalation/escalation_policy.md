# NimbusCart Escalation Policy (internal)

## When to escalate to human support
Escalate if ANY of these is true:
- Urgency is High.
- The customer is angry.
- No matching policy document was found, or the category is Other.
- The complaint involves a legal threat, fraud or a payment dispute.
- The Resolution Agent's confidence is below 0.6.

## Who handles escalations
- Payment disputes and duplicate charges: Billing team, within 4 business hours.
- Fraud or unauthorised activity: Trust and Safety team, within 2 hours.
- Legal threats: Customer Relations lead, the same business day.
- No policy found: Support supervisor, within 24 hours.
- Other angry or high-urgency cases: Senior support agent, within 4 hours.

## Writing the escalation reason
- Short and specific, joined with " + ". Example: "High urgency + Angry customer".

## Ticket status values
- Resolved: the reply can be sent automatically.
- Escalated: a human must review the case and the draft reply.
