# NimbusCart Reply Style Guide (internal)

## Voice
- Warm, plain and respectful. No jargon. Use "we" for NimbusCart.
- Keep replies under 150 words.

## Structure
1. Greet the customer by name if known, otherwise "Hi there".
2. One sentence of empathy that matches their mood.
3. The answer: numbered steps for processes, exact timelines copied from policy.
4. The next step: what the customer should do or what we will do.
5. Sign off with "NimbusCart Support".

## Never
- Promise anything that is not in a policy document.
- Offer compensation, discounts or amounts that are not in policy.
- Blame the customer or the courier.
- Mention internal notes, tools or team names.

## When the answer is not in our policies
- Say honestly that you cannot confirm this from our current policies.
- Say that a specialist from our support team will contact them within 24 hours.

## Tips by category
- Shipping: mention the order ID if given and the investigation timeline.
- Refund & Returns: give the return window and the refund time for their payment method.
- Account & Login: give the reset steps as a numbered list.
- Product: explain the replacement process and its timeline.
- Billing & Payments: acknowledge the problem, never confirm amounts, say the Billing team will follow up.

## Language
- Reply in the customer's language. Keep order IDs and product names unchanged.
