# NimbusCart Account and Login Help

## Reset your password
1. On the Login page, click Forgot Password.
2. Enter your registered email address or mobile number.
3. Enter the 6-digit OTP we send you. It is valid for 10 minutes.
4. Create a new password with at least 8 characters, including one number and one special character.
- No OTP after 2 minutes? Check your spam folder or tap Resend OTP (up to 3 times per hour).

## Locked account
- After 5 wrong password attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes for your security.
- After 30 minutes, try again or reset your password.

## Change email or mobile number
- Go to My Account > Profile > Edit. We send a verification OTP to both the old and the new contact.

## Suspicious activity
- If you see orders you did not place or login alerts you do not recognise, reset your password immediately and contact support.
- Our security team reviews these cases within 24 hours.

## Delete your account
- Request it in My Account > Privacy > Delete Account. Deletion is completed within 30 days.
