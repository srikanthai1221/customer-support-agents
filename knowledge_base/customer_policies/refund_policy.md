# NimbusCart Returns and Refund Policy

## Return window
- Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, with original tags and packaging.
- Not returnable: innerwear, personal-care products and personalised items.

## How to request a return
1. Go to My Account > Orders and select the item.
2. Click Return Item and choose a reason.
3. Choose free doorstep pickup (available for most PIN codes) or drop-off at a partner store.
4. Pickup is scheduled within 2 business days.

## Refund timelines
- The refund is issued within 2 business days after the item reaches our warehouse and passes a quality check.
- Card or net banking: 5-7 business days to show in your account.
- UPI: 2-3 business days. NimbusCart Wallet: instant.
- Cash on Delivery orders: refunded to your NimbusCart Wallet or by bank transfer (NEFT), your choice.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
- Report the problem within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the item and the packaging.
- We offer a free replacement or a full refund, with no return shipping cost.
- The replacement ships once the pickup of the faulty item is completed.

## Exchanges
- Size or colour exchanges are free, once per order, within 30 days of delivery.
