# NimbusCart Product FAQ

## Item arrived broken or not working
- Report it within 7 days of delivery in My Account > Orders > Report a Problem, with photos or a short video.
- We arrange a free pickup and ship a replacement within 3-5 business days after pickup.
- If the item is out of stock, you get a full refund instead.
- After 7 days, faults are handled under the manufacturer warranty.

## Warranty
- Electronics and kitchen appliances carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage or misuse.
- To claim, go to My Account > Orders > Warranty Claim. The invoice is attached automatically.

## Size and fit
- Every clothing and footwear page has a size chart.
- If the size is wrong, you can exchange it free of charge once (see the Returns and Refund Policy).

## Genuine products
- All products come directly from brands or their authorised distributors.

## Out of stock items
- Tap Notify Me on the product page to get an email when it is back in stock.
