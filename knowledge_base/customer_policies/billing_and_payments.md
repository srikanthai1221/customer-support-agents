# NimbusCart Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods
- UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, NimbusCart Wallet, and Cash on Delivery for orders up to Rs 10,000.

## Money debited but no order confirmation
- If money was debited but you received no order confirmation, the amount is reversed automatically within 5-7 business days.

## Duplicate or multiple charges
- An extra charge is usually a temporary bank authorisation. Banks release it within 7 business days.
- If the extra charge is still shown as settled after 7 business days, our Billing team investigates it.
- Duplicate-charge and payment-dispute cases are always handled by the Billing team, not by automated replies.

## Invoices
- Download your GST invoice from My Account > Orders > Download Invoice.

## Coupons
- One coupon per order. Coupons cannot be applied after the order is placed.
