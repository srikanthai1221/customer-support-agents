# NimbusCart Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days in metro cities, 5-7 business days elsewhere in India.
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days in metro cities. It costs Rs 99 extra and is free on orders above Rs 1,999.
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a business day are dispatched the same day.

## Tracking your order
- A tracking link is sent by email and SMS as soon as the order ships.
- You can also track it in My Account > Orders > Track Order.
- Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch.

## Late or missing deliveries
- If your order has not arrived 2 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support with your order ID.
- We open a courier investigation within 24 hours and share an update within 48 hours.
- If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
- If an Express order arrives late, the Express fee is refunded automatically.

## Changing the address and failed deliveries
- You can change the delivery address free of charge until the order is dispatched.
- The courier makes 3 delivery attempts. After that, the parcel returns to our warehouse and is refunded within 7 business days of arrival.

## Shipping charges
- Standard shipping is free on orders above Rs 499. Below that, it costs Rs 49.
